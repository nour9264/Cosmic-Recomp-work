## Alpha 8 — Reworked Options, 120 FPS & Enhanced Cinematics

- **In-game Options:** redesigned the Options hub and the Gameplay, Sound, and Video pages. Gameplay groups the standard and extras settings on one page. Video combines display, button icons, and graphics. Credits are available from the main-menu Options hub.
- **Video and performance:** added a 120 FPS frame-limit option and individual controls for internal resolution, anti-aliasing, texture filtering, sharpening, and screen bloom.
- **Launcher:** added Gameplay settings shared with the game, separate artwork for Home and Settings, and installed-content checks for Four Arms and the enhanced cinematic pack.
- **Enhanced Cinematics:** optional in-game playback of 19 clips in 1080p/60 FPS MP4, with subtitle tracks, keyboard/controller skip, and volume linked to the game's Windows Volume Mixer setting. Enable it under Launcher Settings → Video after importing the ZIP. Turn it off to use the original cinematics. [Download the Enhanced Cinematics ZIP](https://www.mediafire.com/file/n3khvhlbnqgrqiy/CosmicRecomp-Enhanced-Cinematics-1080p60-MP4.zip/file).
- **Menu fixes:** improved Albedo theme colors and pause-menu row spacing, and restored the main-menu Ultimatrix backdrop after using Cheats.

**Known issues:** 120 FPS depends on the scene and hardware, with drops reported in The Amazon. The crank prompt above Humungousaur can still show the Xbox icon under PlayStation or Keyboard. A gameplay crash is still under investigation.
